About Us

Why Content Creation + AI, together.

We started LocalEyes because most agencies force a trade-off between authenticity and speed. We didn't think you should have to choose.

Most agencies sell the same templated ad campaign, then disappear. We started LocalEyes because your business isn’t generic — and your marketing shouldn’t be either.

Every voiceover, script, and visual is built from scratch, launched fast, and measured obsessively. LocalEyes is built for business owners who already have a great product — and are done getting mediocre marketing.

Our Philosophy

Authenticity and speed aren't opposites

Real creators build trust. AI video builds volume. Most agencies pick one — we run both, on purpose.

Content creation brings the trust

A real person talking to camera converts cold audiences better than polished brand video ever will.

  • Builds credibility with new-to-brand audiences
  • Feels native to social feeds, not like an ad
  • Backed by a network of vetted creators
AI brings the speed

You can't out-test a competitor if production takes three weeks per concept. AI video closes that gap.

  • New hooks and variations in 48–72 hours
  • Lower cost per asset for high-volume testing
  • Keeps creative fresh as fatigue sets in
