FAQ

Common questions

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Packages run month-to-month. We ask for a 90-day minimum on paid ads specifically, since that's roughly how long it takes to get a statistically meaningful read on performance.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No — the package fee covers strategy, production, management, and reporting. Ad spend is billed directly by the platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube) so you always see exactly where your budget is going.

Can we mix UGC and AI video within a package?

Yes — every package's content volume can be split between real creator UGC and AI-generated video however makes sense for your goals and budget.

What if we only need one service, not a full package?

That's fine. Packages are the most common setup, but we also work à la carte on individual services like paid ads only, or content production only. Book a call and we'll scope it.

Not sure which package fits?

Book a free strategy call and we'll recommend the right tier for your budget and goals.
