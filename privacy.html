Privacy Policy.

What we collect when you use this site, why, and how it's protected.

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how LocalEyes ("LocalEyes," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit thelocaleyes.net (the "Site") or submit information through it. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly. When you submit the contact form to request a strategy call or ask about our services, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your business name
  • Your phone number, if you choose to provide it
  • The service you're interested in and your budget range
  • Any message or details you include

We do not currently collect payment or financial information through this Site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and follow up about our services
  • Prepare a custom growth plan or proposal, if requested
  • Maintain a record of leads and client communication
  • Improve our services and how we communicate with prospective clients

We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing outside of following up on your own inquiry.

How Your Information Is Stored

Form submissions are stored in a hosted database (Supabase, built on PostgreSQL), protected by row-level security policies that restrict access to approved LocalEyes team members only. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

Third-Party Services We Use

Running this Site relies on a small number of infrastructure providers, each of which may process standard technical information (like IP address or request metadata) as part of delivering their service:

  • Vercel — hosts the Site and its content
  • Supabase — stores form submissions and powers our internal admin tools
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this Site
  • jsDelivr — delivers a JavaScript library used to communicate with our database

These providers have their own privacy practices governing the technical data they process on our behalf.

Cookies

This Site does not currently set cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. If that changes — for example, if we add analytics in the future — this policy will be updated first.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you've submitted at any time by emailing hello@localeyes.agency. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business owners and professionals. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices or services change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach out at hello@localeyes.agency.
