Content Creation & AI Video

Get content that converts, produced two ways.

Real creators for authenticity and trust. AI-generated video for speed and volume. We use both, deliberately, depending on what the campaign needs.

Why Both

Two production methods, one strategy

Neither replaces the other — they solve different problems.

Real Content Creation

Best for trust, authenticity, and content that has to feel like a real person talking.

  • Testimonial-style ads that build trust fast
  • Higher perceived authenticity in-feed
  • Great for new-to-brand cold audiences
  • Ideal for services where trust drives the decision
AI-Generated Video

Best for speed, volume, and testing more hooks than a production schedule allows.

  • Multiple hooks and variations in 48–72 hours
  • Lower cost per asset for high-volume testing
  • Great for product-led, visual-first offers
  • Ideal when creative fatigue needs constant refresh
Our Process

From brief to delivered asset

Same rigor, whether a human or Higgsfield is producing the footage.

1

Brief

We define the hook, angle, and talking points — and decide whether content creation, AI video, or both fit the goal.

2

Creator Shoots / AI Generates

A matched creator films on location, or we run the brief through Higgsfield to generate variations.

3

Review

Our team reviews every asset for quality and on-brand fit before it reaches you.

4

Delivered

Approved assets land in your content library, ready to run in paid or organic.
